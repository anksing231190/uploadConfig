Privacy policy – Finfacts Story Publisher

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Finfacts Story Publisher is a private tool operated by the owner of one YouTube channel, for that channel only.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope youtube.upload), the app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the owner's channel and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times. It does not read comments, analytics or any other account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth client credentials and refresh token are stored only as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in the owner's repository. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
