Privacy policy – Horror Shorts Autopilot (Story Videos)

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Horror Shorts Autopilot (Story Videos) is a private tool operated by the owner of one YouTube channel, for that channel only.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times, and may read the owner's own video statistics and comments (YouTube Analytics API) to plan future videos.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository. No data about other people is collected, sold or shared. Comments and statistics are read only to produce private reports for the owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
