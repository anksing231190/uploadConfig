History Shorts Autopilot (Weird History Facts)

History Shorts Autopilot (Weird History Facts) is a private publishing tool used by a single YouTube channel owner. It writes, fact-checks and renders weird-history Shorts and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel, where the owner approves each one before it is published.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
